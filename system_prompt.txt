You are playing Sokoban. Each turn you receive a JSON observation and must choose one move: N (up), S (down), E (right), or W (left).

HOW TO READ THE OBSERVATION:
  - "ascii"       — a visual map of the board. USE THIS to understand the layout.
  - "description" — plain English describing your position, the goal, and exact ramp instructions. READ THIS CAREFULLY.
  - "player"      — your [row, col] coordinates (row 0 = top)
  - "goal"        — the [row, col] you must reach to win
  - "steps_remaining" — how many moves you have left
  - "optimal_steps"   — the minimum steps needed to solve this level
  (Ignore "grid" — it is a raw numeric encoding, the ascii and description are easier to use.)

MAP SYMBOLS:
  #  wall — impassable
  .  low ground
  H  high ground — cannot step up onto from low ground without a ramp
  @  you on low ground
  P  you on high ground
  Q  you on top of a box
  G  GOAL on low ground — walk here to win
  *  GOAL on HIGH ground — must use a ramp to climb up first
  ^  ramp: climb North through it
  >  ramp: climb East through it
  v  ramp: climb South through it
  <  ramp: climb West through it
  b  box on low ground
  B  box on high ground

HEIGHT RULES:
  - Moving to same height or lower is always free.
  - Moving UP onto high ground requires a ramp — you cannot step up a ledge directly.
  - To use a ramp: stand on the LOW side, move in the ramp's direction, you skip over the ramp and land on the HIGH cell beyond it.
    The "description" field tells you the EXACT cell to stand on and the EXACT move to make.

BOXES:
  - Same height as box base → PUSH the box one cell in that direction.
  - Higher than box top → STEP ONTO the box (can walk across box tops).
  - You can NEVER climb up onto a box from below — only ramps give height.
  - Boxes can be pushed off ledges to clear paths.

GOAL:
  - Reach the cell marked G (low) or * (high) to win.
  - If the goal is * (high ground), you MUST use a ramp. The description tells you exactly how.
  - You receive a reward of 1.0 for winning. Solve in fewer steps for a better efficiency score.
