You are playing Sokoban. Each turn you receive the current board state and must choose one move: N (up), S (down), E (right), or W (left).

MAP SYMBOLS:
  #  wall — impassable
  .  low ground — you can walk here
  H  high ground — you can walk here IF already at the same height, but cannot step up onto it from low ground without a ramp
  @  you (on low ground)
  P  you (on high ground)
  Q  you (standing on top of a box)
  G  GOAL on low ground — walk here to win
  *  GOAL on HIGH ground — you must use a ramp to climb up before you can reach it
  ^  ramp that lets you climb North
  >  ramp that lets you climb East
  v  ramp that lets you climb South
  <  ramp that lets you climb West
  b  box on low ground
  B  box on high ground

HEIGHT RULES:
  - You can freely move to any cell at the same height or lower.
  - To move UP onto high ground, you MUST use a ramp. You cannot climb a ledge directly.
  - Ramps are on low ground. To use a ramp: approach from the low side, move in the ramp's direction, and you land on the high cell beyond it.
    Example: ramp < at [3,5] (climbs West) — stand at [3,6] and press W — you land on high ground at [3,4].
    Example: ramp ^ at [2,3] (climbs North) — stand at [3,3] and press N — you land on high ground at [1,3].
  - Entering a ramp from the side or behind PUSHES it one cell.

BOXES:
  - If you are at the same height as the box base: pressing into it PUSHES the box one cell.
  - If you are higher than the box top (e.g. on high ground next to a low-ground box): you STEP ONTO the box and can walk across it.
  - You can NEVER climb up onto a box from below.
  - Boxes can be pushed off ledges.

STRATEGY:
  - If the goal (*) is on high ground, find the ramp and use it. The description tells you exactly where to stand.
  - If a box blocks your path, push it out of the way.
  - Plan your path before moving: check the description for exact ramp positions and landing spots.

SCORING: reward = optimal_steps / steps_used. Fewer steps = higher score.
