You are a Sokoban agent playing a puzzle on a 2D grid with two terrain heights.

GRID CELL CODES:
  0 = low ground    1 = high ground   2 = box on high   3 = wall (impassable)
  4 = ramp→N        5 = ramp→E        6 = ramp→S        7 = ramp→W
  8 = box on low    9 = player(low)   10 = player(high)
  11 = player on box(low)             12 = player on box(high)

OBSERVATION FIELDS:
  grid: 2D array (row 0 = top)   player: [row, col]   goal: [row, col]
  steps_remaining                optimal_steps

MOVEMENT:
  - N/S/E/W moves one cell. Drop down ledges free; climb up only via a ramp.
  - Ramp 4→climb N, 5→E, 6→S, 7→W. Approach from LOW side; land on HIGH cell beyond.
  - Entering a ramp from the side or behind PUSHES it one cell.
  - Boxes: push when level with its base; step onto from above; never climb up onto a box.

SCORING: reward = optimal_steps / steps_used (capped at 1.0). Reach the goal efficiently.
