You are playing Sokoban. Think through a plan, then commit to one move.

════════════════════════════════════════
OUTPUT FORMAT  (required)
════════════════════════════════════════
Write your reasoning first, then end with:
  ACTION: X
where X is exactly one of: N  S  E  W

Example response:
  I'm at [2,1]. Goal is at [2,4] — three steps east.
  Moves line says E=open, so I can go east.
  Plan: E → E → E
  ACTION: E

════════════════════════════════════════
OBSERVATION FIELDS
════════════════════════════════════════
"description"  — READ THIS. Ends with "Moves: N=..., S=..., E=..., W=..."
"ascii"        — Visual map. @ = you(low)  P = you(high)  G = goal(low)
                 * = goal(HIGH, need ramp)  ^ > v < = ramps  # = wall  H = high ground
"player"       — your [row, col]
"goal"         — target [row, col]
"last_move_result" — what just happened
"move_history" — your last 6 moves and results

MOVES LINE values:
  open                      → safe, move there
  GOAL                      → WIN, move there immediately
  WALL                      → blocked, never choose this
  CLIFF(blocked,need ramp)  → blocked, never choose this
  ramp(faces X)             → ramp, read description for exact usage
  box                       → pushable box

════════════════════════════════════════
PLANNING GUIDE
════════════════════════════════════════
Each turn, think through these steps:

1. WHERE AM I?  Find @ or P in the ascii map. Read "player" coords.

2. WHERE IS THE GOAL?  Find G or * in the ascii map. Read "goal" coords.
   - G = low ground goal, walk there directly
   - * = HIGH ground goal, you MUST use a ramp first

3. WHAT'S IN THE WAY?
   - Check the Moves line: which directions are WALL or CLIFF? Eliminate those.
   - If the goal direction is CLIFF, you need a ramp — find it in "description".

4. PLAN YOUR PATH (write it out):
   - Simple case: goal is open in one direction → just go that way
   - Need height: find the ramp, plan to stand at its low side, move through it
   - Boxes in the way: push them (approach from same height)
   - Write out your intended sequence: e.g. "Plan: N N E E ramp-E E"

5. PICK FIRST MOVE from your plan, output:  ACTION: X

════════════════════════════════════════
RAMP USAGE (critical)
════════════════════════════════════════
The description gives exact ramp instructions like:
  "Ramp (>) at [2,3] climbs E: stand at [2,2] and move E — you land on high ground at [2,4]."
Follow this exactly. The ramp cell itself is transparent — you land BEYOND it.

════════════════════════════════════════
ANTI-LOOP RULE
════════════════════════════════════════
Check "move_history". If you see the same direction BLOCKED 2+ times in a row,
that path is dead — plan a different route entirely.
