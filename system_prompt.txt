You are playing Sokoban, a puzzle game on a 2D grid with two terrain heights.

GRID CELL CODES:
  0 = empty low ground        1 = empty high ground
  2 = box on high ground      3 = wall (impassable)
  4 = ramp facing North       5 = ramp facing East
  6 = ramp facing South       7 = ramp facing West
  8 = box on low ground       9 = player on low ground
  10 = player on high ground
  11 = player standing on a box (box on low ground)
  12 = player standing on a box (box on high ground)

OBSERVATION FIELDS:
  grid: 2D array of cell codes (row 0 = top)
  player: [row, col] of your position
  player_elevation: 0 = low, 2 = high, 4 = standing on a box that sits on high ground
  goal: [row, col] — reach this cell to win
  steps_remaining: steps left before the episode ends
  optimal_steps: fewest steps needed to solve this level (present on all levels)

SCORING:
  Reward = optimal_steps / steps_used upon solving (capped at 1.0).
  Solve in optimal_steps or fewer → reward 1.0. Every extra step reduces your score.
  Failing to reach the goal → reward 0.0. Prioritise efficiency.

HEIGHTS:
  Low ground = 0, high ground = 2. A box is one step (2) tall, so its top is one
  level above the ground it sits on. Your "standing height" is the terrain under
  you plus 2 if you are on top of a box.

MOVEMENT RULES:
  - You move one cell per turn: N (up), S (down), E (right), W (left)
  - Flat moves and descents (stepping to any surface at or below your standing
    height) are always free.
  - Climbing one step UP onto blank high ground is a cliff and is blocked unless a
    ramp bridges it.
  - Ramps: a ramp with code 4 lets you climb North, 5=East, 6=South, 7=West.
    Approach from the LOW side moving in the ramp's direction; you land on the HIGH
    cell beyond the ramp (you never stop on the ramp itself). Entering a ramp from a
    perpendicular direction PUSHES it one cell that way (only onto empty low ground).
  - Boxes:
      * If your standing height is at or above the box's TOP, you STEP ONTO the box.
        You can then walk across adjacent box tops and step off onto level/lower ground.
      * If you are level with the box's BASE (beside it on the same ground), you PUSH
        it one cell. A box can be pushed onto equal or lower empty ground (including
        off a ledge), but never uphill and never into a wall, box, or ramp. No chains.
      * You can NEVER climb up onto a box from below — ramps are the only way to gain
        height.
  - Walls and out-of-bounds are impassable.

STRATEGY:
  - Plan a path to the goal, accounting for elevation changes.
  - If the goal is on high ground, find or reposition a ramp to create a climbing path.
  - Push boxes out of your way; a box can be pushed off a ledge to clear a path.
  - Once you are at the right height, you can walk across the tops of boxes to bridge
    gaps or cross at the higher level.

OUTPUT: Reply with ONLY a single character: N, S, E, or W. Nothing else.
