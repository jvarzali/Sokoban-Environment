You are playing Mountain Sokoban, a puzzle game on a 2D grid with two terrain heights.

GRID CELL CODES:
  0 = empty low ground        1 = empty high ground
  2 = box on high ground      3 = wall (impassable)
  4 = ramp facing North       5 = ramp facing East
  6 = ramp facing South       7 = ramp facing West
  8 = box on low ground       9 = player on low ground
  10 = player on high ground

OBSERVATION FIELDS:
  grid: 2D array of cell codes (row 0 = top)
  player: [row, col] of your position
  player_elevation: 0=low, 2=high
  goal: [row, col] — reach this cell to win
  steps_remaining: steps left before the episode ends

MOVEMENT RULES:
  - You move one cell per turn: N (up), S (down), E (right), W (left)
  - Flat moves and descents (high→low) are always free
  - Climbing (low→high) requires a ramp: a ramp with code 4 lets you climb North,
    5=East, 6=South, 7=West. Approach from the LOW side moving in the ramp's direction.
    You land on the HIGH cell beyond the ramp — you never stop on the ramp itself.
  - Entering a ramp from a perpendicular direction PUSHES it one cell in that direction
    (only works if the destination is empty low ground)
  - Entering a box pushes it one cell forward. Boxes can fall off ledges (high→low) but
    cannot be pushed uphill. No chain pushes.
  - Walls and out-of-bounds are impassable.

STRATEGY:
  - Plan a path to the goal, accounting for elevation changes.
  - If the goal is on high ground, find or reposition a ramp to create a climbing path.
  - Push boxes out of your way; a box can be pushed off a ledge to clear a high-ground path.

OUTPUT: Reply with ONLY a single character: N, S, E, or W. Nothing else.
