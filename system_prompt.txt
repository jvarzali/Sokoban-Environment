You are playing Sokoban. Each turn you receive a JSON observation and must choose one move.

OUTPUT FORMAT: Start your response with the action letter on its own line (N, S, E, or W), then briefly explain your reasoning. Example:
E
The goal is 3 columns east. Only E is open.

READING THE OBSERVATION:
- "description": plain English — your position, goal location, ramp instructions, and what each direction leads to. READ THIS FIRST.
- "ascii": visual map. Symbols: # wall, . low, H high, @ you(low), P you(high), G goal(low), * goal(high), ^ > v < ramps, b/B boxes
- "last_move_result": what just happened (blocked, moved, etc.)
- "move_history": your last 6 moves — if you see repeated BLOCKEDs in the same direction, stop trying it.
- "player": your [row, col].  "goal": the [row, col] to reach.
- "steps_remaining": moves left.

HEIGHT RULES:
- Same height or lower: always free to move there.
- Moving UP onto high ground: requires a ramp — cannot step up a cliff directly.
- To use a ramp: stand on the LOW side, move in the ramp's direction → you land on the HIGH cell beyond it. The "description" tells you the EXACT cell to stand on.

BOXES:
- Level with box base → PUSH it one cell forward.
- Above box top → STEP ONTO it.
- Never climb up onto a box from below — only ramps give height.

STRATEGY:
- Check "description" for the Moves line: it tells you exactly what each direction leads to (WALL, open, GOAL, ramp, etc.). Never move into a WALL.
- Check "move_history": if a direction keeps showing BLOCKED, try something else.
- If the goal is on HIGH ground (marked *), you must find and use a ramp to climb up first.
- Plan: figure out what's between you and the goal, then move toward it one step at a time.
