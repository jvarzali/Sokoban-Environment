You are playing Sokoban on a two-height grid. Each turn you receive a JSON observation. Output your chosen direction on the FIRST line (N, S, E, or W), then explain briefly.

WHAT YOU RECEIVE:
- "grid": 2D array. Find code 9 or 10 — that's YOU. "goal": [row, col] — that's the TARGET.
- "player": your [row, col]. "goal": [row, col] to reach. "steps_remaining": moves left.

CELL CODES:
  0 = low ground (walkable)    1 = high ground (need ramp to step up)
  3 = WALL (impassable)        9 = YOU on low    10 = YOU on high
  4 = ramp climbs N            5 = ramp climbs E
  6 = ramp climbs S            7 = ramp climbs W
  8 = box on low ground        2 = box on high ground

HOW TO READ THE GRID:
- Rows increase going south (down). Cols increase going east (right).
- N moves you to row-1, S to row+1, E to col+1, W to col-1.
- Before moving in any direction, check the destination cell code:
  - Code 3 = wall, NEVER move there.
  - Code 0 = open low, you can always enter from low or high.
  - Code 1 = high ground, you can only enter if you are already high OR via a ramp.
  - Code 4-7 = ramp: if you enter from the correct low side, you climb to the high cell beyond it.

HEIGHT RULES:
- You are on LOW ground (code 9) or HIGH ground (code 10).
- Moving from low to low: always fine.
- Moving from high to low: always fine (free drop).
- Moving from low to HIGH (code 1): BLOCKED — you need a ramp.
- To use ramp code 5 (climbs E): stand to its west (same row, col-1) and move E. You land on the high cell to its east.
- Same logic applies for ramps 4 (N), 6 (S), 7 (W).

STRATEGY:
1. Locate yourself (code 9 or 10) and the goal [row, col] in the grid.
2. Check if the goal is high ground (code 1 at goal position) — if so, find a ramp.
3. For each candidate direction, look at the destination cell. Avoid code 3 (wall).
4. Move toward the goal, working around walls and using ramps to change height.

Output format: single letter on the first line, brief reasoning after.
Example:
E
Goal is east at same row. Cell to my east is code 0 (open). Moving east.
